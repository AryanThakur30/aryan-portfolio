About Me

I am a 4th-year student from Delhi University pursuing B.Sc. (Hons) Computer Science, focused on building practical, real-world projects in data analytics and web development.

I enjoy working on problems where data, logic, and systems come together. My interest lies in understanding how applications work end-to-end, from design and development to deployment.

Currently, I am sharpening my skills by building and deploying projects rather than stopping at theory. I believe in learning by doing and improving through iteration.
